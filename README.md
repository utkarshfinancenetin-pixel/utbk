# Utkarsh Finance

Official web portal for **Utkarsh Finance** (`utkarshfinance.net.in`).
**Tagline:** आपकी उम्मीद का फाइनेंस (Aapki Umeed Ka Finance)

## Features
- **Utkarsh Brand Palette:** Corporate Deep Navy Blue (`#0A3871`) and Vibrant Utkarsh Orange (`#F37022`).
- **Comprehensive Loan Products:** Personal Loans, Home Loans, MSME Loans, Business Loans, Loan Against Property (LAP), Vehicle Loans, and Education Loans.
- **Interactive EMI Calculator:** Real-time loan repayment calculation with sliders for principal, rate, and tenure.
- **Online Loan Application:** Fast, digital application form with simulated application reference generation.
- **Direct Contact & Compliance:** Dedicated customer care toll-free lines (`1800 123 9878`), official email (`utkarshfinance.net.in@gmail.com`), and Varanasi corporate head office details.
